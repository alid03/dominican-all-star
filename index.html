More Than a Cut. It's a Culture

The moment you walk in, you know the energy, music playing, good atmosphere in the room, and the fresh smell of product in the air. Allstar Dominican is the kind of spot people in the Atlanta area come to when they want a clean cut from someone they know will take care of them. No appointments. No waiting. Just walk in, sit down, and get taken care of.

The shop was founded on a commitment to excellence and authentic craftsmanship. Over time, we built a strong team of experienced barbers who bring their own unique style. The standard stays the same: clean work, good service, and a hot towel finish with every cut.

And right inside the same space, Allstar Dominican Hair Salon brings that same energy to full hair care, from blowouts and color to treatments and styling.
