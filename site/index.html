Marietta's Go-To Barbershop

More Than a Cut. It's a Culture

The moment you walk in, you know the energy, music playing, good atmosphere in the room, and the fresh smell of product in the air. Allstar Dominican is the kind of spot people in the Atlanta area come to when they want a clean cut from someone they know will take care of them. No appointments. No waiting. Just walk in, sit down, and get taken care of.

The shop was founded by Adrian, an amazing and experienced barber known for his natural talent and attention to detail. Over time, he built a strong team around him, Juan, Kelvin, Felipe, Suerto, Noel, and more. Every barber brings their own style, but the standard stays the same: clean work, good service, and a hot towel finish with every cut.

And right next door in the next suite, Glam Dominican Salon brings that same energy to full hair care, from blowouts and color to treatments and styling.
